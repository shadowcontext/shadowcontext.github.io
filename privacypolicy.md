**Last updated: 21 July 2026**

ShadowContext is an independent cybersecurity publication. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit `shadowcontext.com`, contact us, or follow links from the site.

## Information processed when you visit

ShadowContext does not require an account, subscription, or payment to read the site. We do not operate a visitor database or intentionally collect personal information simply because you read an article.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. When a GitHub Pages site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address for security purposes. GitHub may also process ordinary technical request information under its own privacy terms. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [GitHub Pages data-collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

The site loads DM Mono and Manrope through the Google Fonts Web API. To deliver those fonts, the visitor's browser requests files from Google. Google says these requests include the visitor's IP address, the requested URL, and HTTP headers such as browser and operating-system information and the referring page. Google also says Google Fonts does not set or log cookies and does not use this information to profile visitors or for targeted advertising. See [Google Fonts privacy and data collection](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

## Information you choose to provide

If you email us, we receive the email address, message, and any other information you include. We use it to respond, evaluate tips or corrections, maintain editorial correspondence, protect the site, and meet legal obligations where applicable.

Please do not send passwords, credentials, unnecessary personal data, confidential records, active exploit material, or information you are not authorized to disclose. ShadowContext is an editorial publication and not a vulnerability-disclosure intake service.

## Cookies, analytics, and advertising

As of the date above, ShadowContext does not use first-party cookies, advertising trackers, behavioral profiling, or an active web-analytics service. Comments and on-site account features are not enabled. Google Fonts does not set or log cookies through its Web API, according to Google.

If these practices materially change, this policy will be updated before or when the new processing begins, as appropriate.

## How information is used and shared

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is used only as reasonably necessary to:

- operate, secure, and maintain the site;
- respond to messages, tips, and correction requests;
- exercise or defend legal rights and comply with applicable law; and
- prevent fraud, abuse, or threats to the service or others.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or communications providers, such as GitHub for site hosting and the provider that handles email. It may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the site. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Retention and security

Email correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose of the exchange, editorial records, security, or applicable legal requirements. Hosting and font-request data is retained by the relevant third-party provider according to its policies.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to a small editorial website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with you, including requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

To make a privacy request, email [shadowcontext@gmail.com](mailto:shadowcontext@gmail.com) with the subject **Privacy request**. We may need to verify the request and may retain limited information when required by law, for security, or to establish or defend legal claims. Data controlled solely by a third-party provider should be addressed directly to that provider.

You can avoid providing correspondence data by not emailing us. Browser controls, content blockers, or disabling remote fonts can limit requests to third-party services, although the site may display differently.

## Children

ShadowContext is written for cybersecurity professionals and general adult audiences. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

## External links

Articles link to external sources for evidence and further reading. ShadowContext does not control those websites or their privacy practices. Review the privacy notice of any external service you visit.

## Changes and contact

We may revise this policy to reflect changes to the site, its providers, or applicable requirements. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to [shadowcontext@gmail.com](mailto:shadowcontext@gmail.com).
